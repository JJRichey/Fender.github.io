Fender v1.0

About & Help

Route Planner

Adding Waypoints: Click anywhere on the map to add waypoints. They'll be numbered sequentially and connected by a coral dashed line showing your planned route.

Navigation Data: Each waypoint shows the distance in nautical miles to the next waypoint.

Total Distance: The route plan displays cumulative distance for provisioning and planning.

Registration Tracker

Track registration requirements for each state and county you visit. Record:

• State and county
• Registration deadline and grace period
• Calendar or business day grace period
• Residency restrictions (multiple types supported)
• Registering authority contact information

All data saves locally to your browser and syncs when you download.

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally in your browser using local storage. Nothing is sent to any server. Your passage plans and registration data remain private and available only to you.
